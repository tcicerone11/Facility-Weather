Facility Weather Operations

Weather translated into operational awareness for facility managers.
TEST SYSTEM. The thresholds on this dashboard are examples for development and testing. They are not approved operating, restriction, or closure policy.

Status Guide

NORMAL Normal operations. No configured weather threshold currently suggests an operational change.
WATCH Weather could affect operations. Monitor conditions and prepare staff or equipment.
ACTION A significant condition has been reached or is expected. Take the protective action in the facility plan.
CLOSE CRITERIA MET A configured closure threshold or critical warning has been reached. Follow approved closure or emergency procedures.
This system uses exact facility coordinates for NWS point forecasts and point-based alerts, plus the configured broader NWS zone for additional context. Airport facilities can also use airport observations and airport short-term forecasts. This dashboard does not provide live lightning-strike distance.
